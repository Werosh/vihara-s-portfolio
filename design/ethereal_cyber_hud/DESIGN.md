## Brand & Style

This design system reimagines high-performance tactical sci-fi interfaces through a refined, ethereal lens. Rooted in cinematic cyberpunk telemetry and AAA game menu architecture, it replaces aggressive harsh neon strobes with soft, diffused bioluminescence and sophisticated tonal nuance.

The visual tone communicates technical precision, quiet mastery, artistic depth, and futuristic elegance. Designed for high-end creative technologist portfolios, AI research environments, and immersive digital command dashboards, the interface establishes a deep obsidian spatial void illuminated by mist-like lavender refractions, periwinkle highlights, and crystalline glassmorphic surfaces.

Stylistically, this system is characterized by:
- **Atmospheric Cyber-HUD:** Razor-sharp condensed headings and utilitarian micro-tracked telemetry anchored in sprawling, cinematic negative space.
- **Diffuse Ethereal Luminescence:** Soft-focus ambient halos, layered translucent glass containers, and mist-like optical blooms rather than harsh laser borders.
- **Modern Feminine Nuance:** An infusion of velvet lavenders, pale lilacs, and muted periwinkles that soften technical austerity into elevated luxury.

## Colors

The color architecture is built natively for deep-space dark mode, pairing infinite obsidian surfaces with delicate, luminous tonal blooms.

### Core Hierarchy
- **Primary (`#B8A9E6`)**: Luminous soft lavender. Deployed across active HUD pill indicators, glowing primary callouts, interactive focuses, and prominent status triggers.
- **Secondary (`#7D72B5`)**: Deep velvet violet. Anchors secondary interaction fills, backplate frames, and structural ambient gradients.
- **Tertiary (`#9D8EC2`)**: Balanced lilac accent. Serves as supporting telemetry highlights, subtle icon fills, and intermediate state treatments.
- **Neutral (`#10131c` / `#0A0E16`)**: Deep obsidian canvas foundation. Absorbs harsh glare and provides deep chromatic contrast for diffused emissive elements.

### Atmospheric Accents
Periwinkle mist (`#ADC6FF`) and lavender radiance are blended into radial ambient layers behind cards and imagery, providing depth without compromising typographic contrast. High-legibility monochrome tones (`#F2F0F8` for headers; `#9D9BB0` for telemetry metadata) ensure effortless readability against glass overlays.

## Typography

The typography unites cinematic, condensed titles with crystalline geometric readability and precise tactical system readouts.

### Hierarchy & Style Rules
- **Display & Headlines (`Bebas Neue`)**: Bold, tall, uppercase titles evoking the scale of theatrical game chapter titles and tactical heads-up interfaces. Always set with deliberate positive letter-spacing to preserve optical air between condensed glyphs.
- **Narrative & Body (`Montserrat`)**: Modern geometric sans-serif that balances modern digital elegance with prolonged reading comfort across project narratives, case study briefs, and technical bios.
- **Data & Telemetry (`Inter`)**: Strictly uppercase with expanded letter-spacing (up to `0.18em`). Utilized for HUD status stamps, system coordinates, tags, metrics, and navigation labels to ensure immediate recognition at micro scales.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a fluid, highly structured 12-column grid balanced by expansive breathing room to support theatrical immersion.

### Breakpoint Matrix
- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column layout with `gutter-lg` (2rem) and `margin-lg` (4rem). Permits floating HUD panels, expansive multi-column metrics, and offset narrative blocks without visual overcrowding.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with `gutter` (1.5rem) and `margin` (2rem). Content cards collapse into 4-column balanced pairs.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column column-first layout with `gutter-sm` (1rem) and `margin-sm` (1.25rem). Pill bars become fixed bottom docks or horizontally swipeable capsules.

### Spacing Cadence
Spacing follows an 8px rhythmic module. Element-level clusters use `space-xs` and `space-sm`, component interiors utilize `space-md` to `space-lg`, and major cinematic section dividers utilize `space-xl` and above to retain chapter-like pacing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is created via layered dark translucent planes, gossamer-thin edges, and wide-radius ethereal glow halos instead of hard-edged drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Depth Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Obsidian Void)**: Background foundation `#0A0E16` to `#10131C`. Static ambient background blurs (`radial-gradient` of `#7D72B5` at 8% opacity with 120px blur) provide subtle environmental warmth behind key profile subjects.
- **Tier 1 (Tactical Glass)**: Semi-transparent backdrop panels (`rgba(16, 19, 28, 0.72)`) backed by `backdrop-blur(16px)`. Enclosed by an ultra-fine border (`1px solid rgba(184, 169, 230, 0.16)`).
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Floating Elements)**: Lifted cards and active drawer overlays. Supported by a subtle ambient drop shadow: `0 12px 32px rgba(10, 14, 22, 0.75)`.
- **Tier 3 (Ethereal Luminescence)**: Active interactive controls, selected pill navigators, and focused triggers. Highlighted by diffused multi-stop lavender drop glows: `0 0 24px rgba(184, 169, 230, 0.28), 0 0 60px rgba(125, 114, 181, 0.15)`. Sharp, laser-like strokes are avoided in favor of wide, atmospheric light diffusion.

## Shapes

The interface blends ergonomic geometric chassis containers with sleek interactive pill contours.

### Shape Hierarchy
- **Card Containers & Modules**: Utilize Level 2 roundedness (`0.5rem` / 8px for standard tiles, up to `1rem` / 16px for prominent section surfaces). This preserves the structured, high-tech architectural feel of digital HUDs.
- **Navigation, Badges, and Action Triggers**: Fully rounded pill forms (`rounded-full` / 9999px). The continuous curve softens technical density, offering a tactile and graceful visual contrast against boxy grid containers.
- **Backdrop Halo Slabs**: Softened organic masks or rounded polygon backplates positioned behind focal illustrations and profile cutouts to isolate silhouettes.

## Components

### HUD Navigation Pill Bar
- **Construction**: Centered floating capsule (`rounded-full`) composed of `rgba(16, 19, 28, 0.8)` glass with `backdrop-blur(16px)` and a `1px` stroke of `rgba(184, 169, 230, 0.2)`.
- **Active Segment**: Filled with luminous soft lavender (`#B8A9E6`), high-contrast dark text (`#0A0E16`), and an ethereal drop glow (`0 0 20px rgba(184, 169, 230, 0.35)`).
- **Inactive Segments**: Muted lavender-slate label (`#9D9BB0`) transitioning to bright lilac-white (`#F2F0F8`) on hover without background fill.

### Buttons & Action Controls
- **Primary Pill**: Background `#B8A9E6` with crisp `#0A0E16` uppercase typography (`Inter` bold). Hover induces a soft blooming glow (`0 0 24px rgba(184, 169, 230, 0.45)`) and a subtle scale elevation (`1.02`).
- **Secondary Ghost Action**: Pill silhouette encased in a `1px` border of `rgba(184, 169, 230, 0.35)`, transparent background, and `#F2F0F8` text. Hover fills the interior with `rgba(184, 169, 230, 0.12)` and illuminates the border to solid `#B8A9E6`.

### Tactical Glass Cards
- **Structure**: Surface fill `rgba(16, 19, 28, 0.65)` layered over `backdrop-blur(14px)` with a perimeter outline of `rgba(184, 169, 230, 0.14)`.
- **Interaction**: On pointer hover, the outer stroke brightens to `rgba(184, 169, 230, 0.45)` accompanied by a faint upward translation (-2px) and a soft ambient lilac glow envelope behind the card base.

### Telemetry Chips & Skill Badges
- **Shape**: Compact `rounded-full` pill capsules.
- **Styling**: `rgba(125, 114, 181, 0.15)` tinted fill, bordered with `rgba(184, 169, 230, 0.25)`. Text is set in tracking-expanded `label-sm` (`#B8A9E6`). Optional dot indicator pulses with an ethereal periwinkle glow.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- **Container**: `rounded-md` (0.5rem) input boxes filled with `rgba(10, 14, 22, 0.6)` and bordered with `rgba(184, 169, 230, 0.2)`.
- **Focus State**: Stroke transitions to `#B8A9E6` flanked by a soft diffused outer field halo (`0 0 16px rgba(184, 169, 230, 0.2)`). Caret and label text switch to bright lilac.

### Toggles, Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Obsidian box with a `1px` lilac border (`rgba(184, 169, 230, 0.3)`). When checked, surface fills with `#B8A9E6` sporting an obsidian check mark and subtle diffused bloom.
- **HUD Switch Toggles**: Pill track in `rgba(16, 19, 28, 0.8)` with a sliding circular thumb. When activated, the track glows `#7D72B5` and the thumb radiates bright `#B8A9E6`.
